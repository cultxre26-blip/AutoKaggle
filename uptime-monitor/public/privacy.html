Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated 5 October 2026. This policy is a template and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction before you rely on it.

("we") is the controller of personal data processed by PingWatch.

What we collect and why

  • Account data: your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself). Used to provide your account, sign you in and send service emails (confirmation, password reset, alerts). Basis: performing our contract with you.
  • Monitoring data: the site names, URLs, keywords and webhook address you enter, and the results of checks (time, status, response time, error type, SSL expiry date). Check results are deleted after 30 days; incident records are kept while your account exists. Basis: performing our contract with you.
  • Billing data: payments are handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and your plan, not your card details.
  • Technical data: request logs (time, path, response status, duration and IP address-based rate limiting) are used to run and secure the Service and are kept for a short period. A session cookie keeps you signed in; we use no advertising or analytics cookies. Basis: our legitimate interest in security and reliability.
  • Human check: if enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile may process your IP address and browser signals when you sign up to prevent abuse.

Who we share data with

Only service providers that help us run PingWatch: our hosting provider, Stripe (payments), our email delivery provider, and Cloudflare (human check, if enabled). Alert webhooks you configure send alert details to the address you provide. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You can download all your data and delete your account from the dashboard at any time. Deleting your account removes your sites, check history and incidents; some records may remain in encrypted backups for up to 14 days. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions and one-time links are stored hashed, connections use HTTPS, and monitoring requests to private network addresses are blocked. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and notify you of material changes.
