Frequently asked questions

What counts as "down"?

A site is marked down after two consecutive failed checks. A failure is a timeout, a connection error, an HTTP status of 400 or above, or (if you set one) a page that is missing your required text.

How often are sites checked?

Free accounts are checked every 5 minutes. Pro and Team accounts can be checked every minute. Checks run from a single location.

Can I monitor private or internal addresses?

No. For security, PingWatch only checks public internet addresses and blocks private, loopback and link-local ranges.

What happens if I cancel or a payment fails?

Your account returns to the Free plan. Monitors above the Free limits stop being checked, but nothing is deleted, and they resume if you subscribe again.

Who can see my data?

Only you. You can download everything we hold about your account or delete it at any time from the dashboard. See the privacy policy for details.
